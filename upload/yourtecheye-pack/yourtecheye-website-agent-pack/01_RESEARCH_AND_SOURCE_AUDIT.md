# 01 — Research & Source Audit

## 1. Research objective

Understand Your Tech Eye's real offer and visual identity, then rebuild its website with a more premium and distinctive design. ECAM (`https://ecam.com/`) is a reference for how to organize a security-services story and move users from interest to a qualified sales enquiry. The output must look and read as Your Tech Eye, not ECAM.

## 3. What the ECAM reference teaches us

Observed at `https://ecam.com/` on 12 October 2026:

1. Start with a clear value proposition, visual hero and a primary “how it works” action.
2. Establish trust early with approved credentials or customers.
3. Introduce core solutions before asking users to understand the technical detail.
4. Explain the intervention process in simple steps.
5. Use measurable proof/case studies only when claims can be substantiated.
6. Explain relevance to different industries or site types.
7. Compare the service model only where a fair, defensible comparison is possible.
8. Answer buyer questions and provide a clear contact route.

### Do not copy

- ECAM logo, trademarks, photos, videos, copy, customer logos, testimonials, metrics, certification marks, visual compositions or proprietary graphics.
- The same black-and-yellow colour system or a pixel-by-pixel section layout.
- ECAM-specific numbers and claims (for example camera counts, crime figures, arrest counts, savings, certifications or “#1” statements).

Use the **sequence and clarity of the buyer journey**, while designing an original experience for YTE.

## 4. Required live-site reconnaissance for the coding agent

Before writing final copy, the agent must:

- Open the root domain in a real browser and record the current status.
- Inspect all visible header/footer navigation links.
- Visit every page linked under Home, About, Services and Contact.
- Follow service detail pages and inspect their headings, body copy, images, video embeds and CTA labels.
- Inspect the logo in header, footer, site icon, social-share metadata and WordPress/media/CMS asset library if available.
- Record exact canonical URLs and important redirects.
- Check whether contact links, WhatsApp links, forms and social profiles work.
- Extract existing company facts, service names and useful copy into a migration inventory before rewriting.
- Save a source URL beside each factual claim in a local `content/source-notes.md` or in the final content document.
- If the website cannot be reached after reasonable retries, continue with only the verified items in this pack and mark uncertain sections as content placeholders for owner review. Do not fabricate service details to make the site look complete.

## 6. Market and trust observations

A surveillance company website earns trust less through dramatic wording and more through clarity: what is monitored, how events are reviewed, what happens next, what the business must provide, where service is available, and whom to contact. The copy should avoid absolute guarantees such as “stops every crime,” “zero incidents,” or “instant response” unless the business has defensible, documented evidence and explicitly approves those claims.
