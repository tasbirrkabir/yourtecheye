# 05 — Content Strategy & Migration

## 1. Source-of-truth hierarchy

Use the following order whenever there is conflicting information:

1. Current official YTE website and its linked service pages.
2. YTE's official CMS/media library and approved documents supplied by the owner.
3. Owner-approved recent social/business profiles.
4. Search-indexed snippets only as leads to investigate, not as final copy when live source is available.
5. External competitor websites only for market context and UX patterns, never as source material for YTE's business facts.

## 2. Migration inventory

Before rewriting, prepare `content/migration-inventory.md` containing:

- Page title, current URL, intended new URL, status.
- H1, title/meta description, major H2 headings and CTA labels.
- Body-copy content worth preserving.
- Linked image/video URLs and local destination filenames.
- Logo/site-icon/social-share image locations.
- Internal links and external profiles.
- Forms and their destinations.
- Claims needing verification.
- Redirect requirement, if any.

For each critical business fact, add a source URL or note “owner confirmation required.”

## 3. Voice and tone

- Calm, clear, professional, specific and reassuring.
- Explain terms like live monitoring or audio intervention in plain English.
- Lead with the customer's operational problem, not technology jargon.
- Prefer “helps detect”, “supports deterrence”, “can enable timely intervention” where accurate over absolute guarantees.
- Avoid fear-based copy, inflated claims and empty phrases such as “world-class cutting-edge revolutionary solutions.”
- Use US/UK spelling consistently based on current YTE copy; record the chosen variant. Do not mix “center/centre” or “organization/organisation” arbitrarily.

## 4. Proposed messaging direction (draft only)

These lines are starting points to validate with YTE, not factual claims:

**Hero headline option A:** “A clearer view of what matters.”

**Hero headline option B:** “Stay aware. Act with confidence.”

**Supporting copy draft:** “Your Tech Eye helps businesses and property teams improve visibility through surveillance and monitoring solutions designed around the needs of each site.”

**Primary CTA:** “Discuss Your Site”

**Secondary CTA:** “Explore Our Services”

Avoid publishing any headline that implies full prevention or guaranteed response. The supporting copy must be adjusted to match the exact service scope.

## 5. Homepage copy framework

### Hero
- One concise headline about visibility/awareness and confidence.
- Supporting sentence naming the verified core offer.
- Primary CTA to contact/enquiry.
- Secondary CTA to service overview.
- Relevant, licensed visual with fallback image.

### Services intro
Explain the range and connect each service to a practical use case. Do not use technical feature lists without explaining their value.

### How it works
Use only the actual operation model. Possible structure (confirm every step):
1. Understand the site's risks, hours and existing equipment.
2. Determine a suitable surveillance/monitoring arrangement.
3. Monitor or review activity based on the agreed service.
4. Follow the documented intervention and escalation plan.

If YTE does not perform any of these steps, remove or correct them.

### Trust section
Good trustworthy proof may include:
- A clear explanation of how monitoring and escalation work.
- Real, approved project photos.
- Genuine team/company identity.
- Actual customer quotes with permission and attribution.
- Verifiable partner/certification evidence.

If this material is not available, use “What to expect” or “A clearer process” instead of fabricated proof.

## 6. Service content template

For each real service, answer:

- What is the service?
- Who is it for?
- What practical problem does it address?
- How does it work in this company's actual operating model?
- What equipment/connectivity/site conditions are required?
- What does the customer need to provide?
- What is included, and what is not included?
- When is it available and in which service areas?
- What happens after an alert?
- What are the limits or assumptions?
- What is the best next step?

If answers are unknown, create an owner-review question. Do not fill the gap with an industry-standard assumption.

## 7. FAQ question bank (answer only after verification)

- What does Your Tech Eye monitor?
- Is the monitoring service available 24/7?
- Does YTE install new cameras or work with an existing system?
- What happens when suspicious activity is detected?
- How does live audio intervention work?
- What equipment or internet connection is required?
- Which areas or locations does YTE serve?
- Is the service suitable for a single property or multiple locations?
- How can I request a site assessment or quote?
- What does the service not cover?

These are question prompts, not approved answers. Remove questions that do not fit the real offer.

## 8. Contact details and CTA consistency

Provisional details surfaced through official-site search snippets:

- Email: `yourtecheye@gmail.com`

Reconfirm both before launch. Use `tel:+8801780145132` and `mailto:yourtecheye@gmail.com` only after verification. A WhatsApp URL may be implemented only if YTE confirms that the number is WhatsApp-enabled and monitored.

## 9. Claims policy

Never invent or imply:
- Number of cameras monitored, incidents prevented, crime reduction, response speed, savings or arrest figures.
- Certifications, licences, compliance badges, awards or official partnerships.
- Customer identities, logos, quotes or case studies.
- Company founding year, employee count, countries/cities served or coverage scale.
- 24/7 availability where the exact service hours or response scope are unclear.
- Law-enforcement dispatch, armed response or emergency-service response if this is not an established, documented feature.

## 10. Metadata and share copy

Draft title pattern: `Your Tech Eye | Surveillance & Monitoring Solutions`

Draft description: `Explore surveillance and monitoring solutions from Your Tech Eye. Learn about available services and discuss the right approach for your site.`

Adjust this after confirming exact offerings and target market. Use the official brand logo or approved social image for Open Graph. Do not use an ECAM asset as a placeholder in production.
